# How Much Do Beauty YouTubers Charge for Sponsorships?

A beauty and fashion channel with 10K to 100K subscribers typically charges about **$420 to $2,300** for a dedicated sponsored video, based on 2025 rate card CPMs of **$28 to $150 per 1,000 views** ([VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/beauty)). These are modeled ranges, not closed deals, so treat them as a starting point for a quote.

## Beauty rates by subscriber count

Modeled dedicated video rate for a US channel at median engagement, from [VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/beauty):

| Subscribers | Example avg views | CPM (per 1,000 views) | Dedicated video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 10K | 3,000 | $18 to $96 | $55 to $290 |
| 10K to 100K | 15,000 | $28 to $150 | $420 to $2,300 |
| 100K to 500K | 60,000 | $38 to $200 | $2,300 to $12,000 |
| 500K to 1M | 150,000 | $48 to $260 | $7,200 to $39,000 |
| 1M+ | 400,000 | $58 to $340 | $23,000 to $136,000 |

The same source says real deals vary 2 to 4 times beyond these ranges depending on exclusivity, usage rights, bundles and timing.

## Why beauty pricing looks like this

Beauty and fashion channels sit at the mid to high end of the CPM range in the VidScored tables, higher than education and lifestyle at every size. Brands in this space buy product-led content, so one video can drive direct sales.

## Who sponsors beauty channels

Cosmetics and skincare brands, fashion and accessories labels, hair tools, and beauty subscription boxes.

## How to price yours

1. Check your average views on the last 10 videos, not your subscriber count.
2. Multiply views by the CPM band for your size, divided by 1,000.
3. Quote the middle of the band, and price usage rights and exclusivity separately.
