# How much do business YouTubers charge for sponsorships?

A business or marketing YouTuber with 10K to 100K subscribers typically charges about $480 to $3,200 for a dedicated sponsored video, based on 2025 rate-card CPMs of $32 to $215 per 1,000 views. Business sits near the top tier because B2B software, course and agency advertisers pay for a professional audience.

## Business rates by subscriber count

Modeled by [VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/business) for a US channel at median engagement:

| Subscribers | Example views | CPM (per 1,000 views) | Dedicated video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 10K | 3,000 | $22 to $145 | $65 to $435 |
| 10K to 100K | 15,000 | $32 to $215 | $480 to $3,200 |
| 100K to 500K | 60,000 | $42 to $290 | $2,500 to $17,000 |
| 500K to 1M | 150,000 | $52 to $365 | $7,800 to $55,000 |
| 1M+ | 400,000 | $62 to $475 | $25,000 to $190,000 |

These are modeled ranges built from IMH, NeoReach and HypeAuditor 2025 rate cards. VidScored says real deals vary 2 to 4 times either way, depending on exclusivity, usage rights and bundles.

## Who sponsors business channels

Per VidScored: marketing and CRM software, course platforms, business tools, hiring services and B2B SaaS.

## How to justify a premium rate

Show audience quality: job role, company size and buying intent. A niche professional audience converts better, which is what supports CPMs well above consumer niches. Price usage rights and exclusivity separately from the base fee.

## FAQ

### How much do business YouTubers charge for a sponsored video?
About $480 to $3,200 at 10K to 100K subscribers, and from about $65 at the smallest sizes to $190,000 or more at 1M+ (modeled, VidScored 2025).

### What sponsors work with business YouTube channels?
Marketing and CRM software, course platforms, business tools, hiring services and B2B SaaS.

### Are business rates higher than other niches?
Yes. Business CPMs of $32 to $215 at mid sizes are well above food ($16 to $88) in the same VidScored dataset.
