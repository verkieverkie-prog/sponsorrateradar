# How Much Do Education YouTubers Charge for Sponsorships?

A education and learning channel with 10K to 100K subscribers typically charges about **$270 to $1,400** for a dedicated sponsored video, based on 2025 rate card CPMs of **$18 to $96 per 1,000 views** ([VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/education)). These are modeled ranges, not closed deals, so treat them as a starting point for a quote.

## Education rates by subscriber count

Modeled dedicated video rate for a US channel at median engagement, from [VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/education):

| Subscribers | Example avg views | CPM (per 1,000 views) | Dedicated video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 10K | 3,000 | $12 to $64 | $36 to $190 |
| 10K to 100K | 15,000 | $18 to $96 | $270 to $1,400 |
| 100K to 500K | 60,000 | $25 to $130 | $1,500 to $7,800 |
| 500K to 1M | 150,000 | $32 to $170 | $4,800 to $26,000 |
| 1M+ | 400,000 | $42 to $220 | $17,000 to $88,000 |

The same source says real deals vary 2 to 4 times beyond these ranges depending on exclusivity, usage rights, bundles and timing.

## Why education pricing looks like this

Education videos tend to have long shelf lives and loyal audiences, but the VidScored tables put their CPMs below beauty and tech at every size.

## Who sponsors education channels

Online learning platforms, study and productivity tools, language apps, and software and book brands aimed at students.

## How to price yours

1. Check your average views on the last 10 videos, not your subscriber count.
2. Multiply views by the CPM band for your size, divided by 1,000.
3. Quote the middle of the band, and price usage rights and exclusivity separately.
