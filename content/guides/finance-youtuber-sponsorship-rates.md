# How much do finance YouTubers charge for sponsorships?

Finance is the best paying niche on YouTube for sponsors. In 2026 most sources put finance sponsorships at **$30 to $60 per 1,000 views**, about 2 to 3 times what a general or entertainment channel gets for the same views.

In dollars that works out to roughly:

- **1K to 10K subs:** $100 to $250 for an integration, $200 to $500 for a dedicated video.
- **50K average views:** about $3,000 for a 30 second integration.
- **100K subs:** $12,000 to $28,000 for a 60 second integration (US audience).
- **200K to 500K subs:** $5,000 to $25,000 for a 60 second integration in reported deals.

Sources for each are below.

## Finance CPM (what sponsors pay per 1,000 views)

| Source | Finance CPM | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-sponsorship-rates/) | $30 to $60 | Personal finance and investing. Dedicated videos get 1.3x to 1.5x |
| [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/how-much-do-youtubers-charge-for-sponsorships/) | $30 to $60 | Finance, investing and crypto are 2x to 3x the base rate |
| [YTCalculators](https://ytcalculators.com/youtube-sponsorship-benchmarks/) | $25 to $35 | Their benchmarks multiply finance by 2.5x over an average niche |

For comparison, OutlierKit puts gaming at $15 to $30 and lifestyle at $15 to $25. Only B2B software beats finance ($40 to $80).

## Small finance channels (1K to 10K subscribers)

From [OutlierKit's nano creator rates](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-nano-influencer-sponsorship-rates/), with a mostly US audience:

| Format | Finance / investing | Lifestyle, for comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated video | $200 to $500 | $75 to $300 |
| Integration | $100 to $250 | $40 to $150 |
| YouTube Short | $50 to $150 | $25 to $80 |

OutlierKit says finance and tech nano channels get 40% to 60% more than lifestyle channels, because the viewers have more buying power and finance advertisers pay more per view.

## Bigger finance channels

- **50,000 average views, US audience:** about $3,000 for a 30 second integration, which is a $60 CPM, per [YTCalculators' benchmarks](https://ytcalculators.com/youtube-sponsorship-benchmarks/).
- **100K subscribers, US audience:** $12,000 to $28,000 for a 60 second integration, per [YTCalculators' niche table](https://ytcalculators.com/blog/how-much-do-youtubers-charge-for-sponsorships/). The same table puts tech at $5,400 to $12,600 and gaming at $3,600 to $8,400.
- **200K to 500K subscribers:** $5,000 to $25,000 per 60 second integration, from publicly reported deals collected by the [same page](https://ytcalculators.com/blog/how-much-do-youtubers-charge-for-sponsorships/).
- **400K views on one video:** $12,000 to $24,000 is a fair integration price for a finance creator, per [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/how-much-do-youtubers-charge-for-sponsorships/), versus $4,000 to $8,000 for a gaming creator with the same views.

The big ranges come from views. A 100K sub channel getting 15K views a video is priced on those 15K views, not on the subscriber count.

## Finance rates on other platforms

- **Instagram:** $40 to $80 per 1,000 impressions for finance and crypto, per [Nowadays Media](https://nowadays.media/influencer-marketing/instagram-influencer-rates-2026/).
- **TikTok:** $30 to $80 per 1,000 views for finance and investing, per [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/tiktok-influencer-pricing/).
- **Newsletters:** about $7.11 per 1,000 subscribers for finance lists, per Paved's Q3 2026 benchmarks [reported by PPC Land](https://ppc.land/paved-finds-hr-newsletter-ad-rates-run-15-times-higher-than-lifestyle-lists/).

## How to price your finance channel

**(Median views per video / 1,000) x $30 to $60 = your integration rate**

- **5,000 median views:** $150 to $300.
- **20,000 median views:** $600 to $1,200.
- **80,000 median views:** $2,400 to $4,800.

For a dedicated video, multiply by about 1.3 to 1.5. Use median views from your last 10 videos, not your best one. If your audience is mostly outside the US, UK and Canada, expect less: OutlierKit uses 0.3x to 0.5x for India and Southeast Asia audiences.

Finance sponsors (brokerages, budgeting apps, tax software) often ask for a promo code or tracked link. A flat fee plus a per signup bonus is common, so get the flat fee in writing first.
