# How much do fitness influencers charge?

Most fitness creators with **10K to 500K followers charge $500 to $5,000 per sponsored post** in 2026, according to [LaunchPoint's fitness rate guide](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-fitness-fitness-influencers-charge). Smaller accounts sit far lower, and big YouTube channels go far higher.

Quick version:

- **Instagram Reel, 10K to 500K followers:** $1,000 to $3,500.
- **TikTok video, 10K to 500K followers:** $800 to $4,000.
- **YouTube dedicated video, 10K to 100K subs:** $270 to $1,500 (modeled), up to $5,000 for a full product review.
- **Fitness CPM on Instagram:** $15 to $35 per 1,000 impressions.

Sources for each are below.

## Fitness podcasts and newsletters

- **Podcasts:** the Health and Fitness category averages a **$24 CPM** per 1,000 downloads, per [Castos](https://castos.com/podcast-advertising-rates/) citing Libsyn data. A show with 5,000 downloads an episode would get about $120 per ad slot.
- **Newsletters:** Health and Wellness newsletters average **$5.60 per 1,000 subscribers** on the Paved marketplace ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/paved-finds-hr-newsletter-ad-rates-run-15-times-higher-than-lifestyle-lists/)). That is one of the lowest categories, so fitness newsletters often do better with affiliate deals.

## How to set your own fitness rate

1. Start from your usual views, not your follower count. Multiply average views per 1,000 by the CPM for your platform above.
2. Charge more for formats that take real work (a full product review or a transformation series) than for a Story.
3. Supplement brands often ask for category exclusivity. Price that on top of the base fee, since it blocks you from other supplement deals.
4. Usage rights (the brand running your video as an ad) are a separate fee, not part of the post price.

## FAQ

### How much does a fitness influencer with 10K followers charge?
Published 2026 rate guides put fitness creators with 10K to 100K followers at about $300 to $2,500 for an Instagram post or Reel and $200 to $2,000 for a TikTok video.

### How much do fitness YouTubers make from a sponsored video?
Modeled 2025 rate-card data puts a dedicated fitness video at about $270 to $1,500 for a 10K to 100K subscriber channel and $1,500 to $8,400 at 100K to 500K subscribers. Views matter more than subscriber count.

### What is a normal CPM for fitness influencers?
About $15 to $35 per 1,000 impressions on Instagram, $20 to $50 per 1,000 views on TikTok, and about $24 per 1,000 downloads for health and fitness podcasts.
