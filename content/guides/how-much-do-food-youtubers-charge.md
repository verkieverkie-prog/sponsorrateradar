# How much do food YouTubers charge for a sponsorship?

A food YouTuber typically charges **$15 to $40 per 1,000 usual views** for a long sponsored video in 2026. On a channel whose videos usually get about 111,000 views, that lands around **$1,700 to $4,500** per video.

Those figures come from [TrendReport](https://trendreport.com/intelligence/youtube-sponsorship-rates-by-niche/), which tracked 4,948 sponsored Food and Drink videos. They are not invoices. TrendReport prices each video from the channel's usual views times a typical rate, because real deals are private. Treat them as a planning band, then check a format-specific quote below.

## Rates by format

[LaunchPoint's 2026 food and beverage rate guide](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-food-beverage-youtubers-charge) breaks the same niche into what brands actually buy. It is a rate guide, not a database of closed deals.

| What you make | Channel size | What to charge |
|---|---|---|
| 60-second mid-roll | 10K - 100K subs | $500 - $3,500 |
| Pre-roll (about 30 seconds) | 100K - 500K subs | $2,500 - $7,500 |
| Dedicated recipe video | 100K - 500K subs | $5,000 - $15,000 |
| Dedicated unboxing or haul | 500K - 1M+ subs | $15,000 - $35,000 |
| Taste-test Short | Size not given | $300 - $1,500 |

LaunchPoint also puts a mid-tier food and beverage YouTuber at a **$15 to $45 CPM**, which matches TrendReport's $15 to $40 band.

A dedicated recipe video costs more than a mid-roll because you are buying groceries, shooting a full cook, and editing an 8 to 12 minute video that is only about the product. A 60-second mention inside a "what I eat in a day" is the cheaper way in.

## How to price your own video

1. Take the median views of your last 10 long videos, not your subscriber count.
2. Multiply by $15 and by $40, then divide by 1,000. That is your TrendReport band. At 20,000 views it is $300 to $800.
3. If the brand wants the whole video (a recipe built around their product), quote toward the dedicated row for your size, not the mid-roll row.
4. Add extra for usage rights, exclusivity, and a rush. LaunchPoint says a strict FDA script on a supplement or energy drink often adds 15 to 20 percent, and a 3 to 6 month exclusivity clause can double the fee.

## FAQ

### How much should a food YouTuber with 10,000 subscribers charge?
LaunchPoint puts a 60-second mid-roll for a 10K to 100K food channel at $500 to $3,500. If your videos only get a few thousand views, start from the $15 to $40 per 1,000 views band instead of the flat fee. 5,000 views is $75 to $200.

### Do food YouTubers get paid more than other niches?
Per video, yes, mostly because food videos get a lot of views. TrendReport's Food and Drink channels usually get about 111,000 views, and a typical sponsored long video there is $1,700 to $4,500. Per 1,000 views, food ($15 to $40) sits in the same band as most other niches. Finance and AI brands pay the top of that band.

### How much is a sponsored food YouTube Short?
LaunchPoint's rate guide puts a taste-test or review Short at $300 to $1,500. It does not say the channel size, so check it against your Shorts views. TrendReport's typical Short rate is $6 to $20 per 1,000 views.
