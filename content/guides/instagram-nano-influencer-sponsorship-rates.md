# Instagram nano influencer sponsorship rates

An Instagram creator with 1,000 to 10,000 followers typically charges $25 to $150 for a sponsored feed post and $50 to $300 for a Reel in 2026. Stories run lower, at $15 to $75. Here are the numbers by format and where each comes from.

## Nano rates by format (1K - 10K followers)

| Format | Typical rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post | $25 - $150 | [Nowadays Media](https://nowadays.media/influencer-marketing/instagram-influencer-rates-2026/) |
| Reel | $50 - $300 | Nowadays Media |
| Reel (survey of nearly 1,200 creators) | $150 - $400, median $250 | [Aspire](https://www.aspire.io/blog/how-much-do-instagram-influencers-charge-for-sponsored-posts) |
| Story | $15 - $75 | Nowadays Media |

Nowadays Media bases its tiers on more than $17M of placed spend. Aspire's survey puts the middle half of nano Reel rates between $150 and $400, with a full range of $10 to nearly $1,000.

## What changes at 10K to 100K followers

| Format | Micro-tier rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post | $150 - $500 | Nowadays Media |
| Reel | $300 - $800 | Nowadays Media |
| Story | $50 - $250 | Nowadays Media |

## How to use these numbers

- Treat them as benchmarks, not closed deals. Engagement and niche move the price a lot.
- Price Reels above feed posts. Brands pay more for video.
- Quote a package (post plus Stories) rather than a single asset.

## FAQ

**How much does a nano influencer charge per Instagram post?** Published 2026 tiers put it at about $25 to $150 for a feed post.

**How much for a Reel?** $50 to $300 in Nowadays Media's tiers, and a survey median of $250 from Aspire.

**When do rates jump?** Around 10K followers, where feed posts start at roughly $150.
