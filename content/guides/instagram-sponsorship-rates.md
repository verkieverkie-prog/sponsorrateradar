# Instagram sponsorship rates

An Instagram creator with 1,000 to 10,000 followers typically gets $25 to $150 for a sponsored feed post and $50 to $300 for a Reel in 2026, according to [Nowadays Media](https://nowadays.media/influencer-marketing/instagram-influencer-rates-2026/), an agency that reports over $17M in placed influencer spend. Reels pay the most at every tier, and Stories the least.

## Rates by follower count

| Followers | Feed post | Reel | Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K - 10K (nano) | $25 - $150 | $50 - $300 | $15 - $75 |
| 10K - 100K (micro) | $150 - $500 | $300 - $800 | $50 - $250 |
| 100K - 500K (mid-tier) | $500 - $2,500 | $800 - $5,000 | $200 - $800 |
| 500K - 1M (macro) | $2,500 - $10,000 | $5,000 - $15,000 | $500 - $2,000 |
| 1M+ (mega) | $10,000 - $50,000+ | $15,000 - $100,000 | $2,000 - $10,000 |

Source: [Nowadays Media, Instagram influencer rates 2026](https://nowadays.media/influencer-marketing/instagram-influencer-rates-2026/). These are industry benchmarks, not individual deals.

## Rates per 1,000 impressions by niche

Your niche changes the price a lot. Finance pays roughly four times what tech and gaming pays per 1,000 impressions:

| Niche | Per 1,000 impressions |
|---|---|
| Finance and crypto | $40 - $80 |
| Beauty and skincare | $20 - $50 |
| Fashion and apparel | $18 - $45 |
| Travel and lifestyle | $15 - $40 |
| Fitness and wellness | $15 - $35 |
| Food and beverage | $12 - $30 |
| Tech and gaming | $8 - $18 |

Source: [Nowadays Media](https://nowadays.media/influencer-marketing/instagram-influencer-rates-2026/).

## How to use these numbers

1. Look up your average Reel impressions over the last 30 days, not your follower count.
2. Multiply by your niche's per-1,000 rate above and divide by 1,000. For example, 20,000 impressions in fitness gives $300 to $700.
3. Check that the result falls inside your follower tier's range. If it's far above, quote the top of the tier range. If it's below, quote the per-impression number.
4. Charge extra for usage rights (the brand running your Reel as an ad) and exclusivity.

## FAQ

### How much should I charge for an Instagram Reel with 10K followers?
Nowadays Media puts micro creators (10K-100K followers) at $300 to $800 per sponsored Reel in 2026.

### Do Instagram Stories pay less than Reels?
Yes. At every tier in the Nowadays Media data, a Story costs a fraction of a Reel, for example $15-$75 versus $50-$300 for nano creators.

### Which Instagram niche pays the most?
Finance and crypto, at $40 to $80 per 1,000 impressions.
