# How much do tech YouTubers charge for a sponsorship?

A tech and gadgets channel with 10K to 100K subscribers typically charges about **$450 to $3,000** for a dedicated sponsored video, based on 2025 rate card CPMs of **$30 to $200 per 1,000 views** ([VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/tech)). Tech sits near the top of the market because review audiences research and buy. These are modeled ranges, not closed deals.

## Tech rates by subscriber count

Modeled dedicated video rate for a US channel at median engagement, from [VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/tech):

| Subscribers | Example avg views | CPM (per 1,000 views) | Dedicated video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 10K | 3,000 | $20 to $138 | $60 to $415 |
| 10K to 100K | 15,000 | $30 to $200 | $450 to $3,000 |
| 100K to 500K | 60,000 | $40 to $275 | $2,400 to $17,000 |
| 500K to 1M | 150,000 | $50 to $350 | $7,500 to $53,000 |
| 1M+ | 400,000 | $60 to $450 | $24,000 to $180,000 |

The same source says real deals vary 2 to 4 times beyond these ranges.

## Who sponsors tech channels

VPNs, software and SaaS tools, web hosts, phone and laptop brands, and accessory makers. High buyer intent keeps tech CPMs among the highest on the platform.

## Small tech channels still get paid well

An engaged, purchase-ready audience matters more to advertisers than raw size, so a small tech channel can still command a strong per video rate.

## How to price yours

1. Check average views on your last 10 videos.
2. Multiply views by the CPM band for your size, divided by 1,000.
3. Quote the middle of the band, and price usage rights and exclusivity separately.
