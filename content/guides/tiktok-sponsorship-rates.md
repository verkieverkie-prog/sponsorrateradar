# TikTok sponsorship rates

A TikTok creator with 1,000 to 10,000 followers usually charges somewhere between $25 and $250 for a sponsored video in 2026. At 10K to 50K followers, published rate tables put it at roughly $150 to $1,500. Here's the full breakdown, and where each number comes from.

## Rates by follower count

| Followers | Sponsored video | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1K - 10K | $50 - $200 | [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/tiktok-influencer-rates/) |
| 1K - 10K | $25 - $150 | [InfluencerFee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/tiktok-influencer-pricing/) |
| 10K - 100K | $200 - $800 | Influencer Marketing Hub |
| 10K - 50K | $150 - $1,500 | InfluencerFee |
| 100K - 1M | $800 - $5,000 | Influencer Marketing Hub |
| 100K - 500K | $1,500 - $4,000 | InfluencerFee |
| 500K - 1M | $4,000 - $20,000 | InfluencerFee |
| 1M+ | $5,000 - $50,000+ | Influencer Marketing Hub |

These are industry benchmarks, not individual deals. Your views per video matter more than your follower count, so check your average views before you quote.

## Dedicated videos and LIVEs cost more

InfluencerFee lists a dedicated, brand-focused video separately from a standard integration:

- 1K - 10K followers: $50 - $250
- 10K - 50K followers: $300 - $2,500
- 100K - 500K followers: $3,000 - $8,000
- A sponsored TikTok LIVE at 10K - 50K followers: $200 - $1,000

## Per-view rates by niche

Some brands pay per view instead. InfluencerFee's cost-per-view benchmarks, shown per 1,000 views:

| Niche | Per 1,000 views |
|---|---|
| Finance / investing | $30 - $80 |
| Beauty / skincare | $20 - $50 |
| Health / wellness | $20 - $50 |
| Fashion / style | $15 - $40 |
| Food / recipe | $10 - $25 |
| Gaming / entertainment | $5 - $15 |

So a finance video averaging 20,000 views could justify roughly $600 to $1,600, while a gaming video with the same views lands closer to $100 to $300.

## How to quote

1. Take your average views over your last 10 videos, not your best one.
2. Multiply by your niche's per-1,000-view range above.
3. Add more for usage rights (the brand running your video as an ad) or exclusivity.

## FAQ

### How much does a TikTok sponsorship pay with 10K followers?
Published rate tables put 10K - 50K follower creators at about $150 to $1,500 per sponsored video, depending on views and niche.

### Do TikTok creators with 1,000 followers get paid by brands?
Yes. Nano creators (1K - 10K followers) are commonly quoted at $25 to $250 per video, often plus free product.

### Which TikTok niche pays the most?
Finance pays the most per view in InfluencerFee's benchmarks, at $30 - $80 per 1,000 views, and gaming the least at $5 - $15.
