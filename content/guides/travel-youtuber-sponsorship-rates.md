# How much do travel YouTubers charge for a sponsorship?

A travel or adventure channel with 10K to 100K subscribers typically charges about **$270 to $1,400** for a dedicated sponsored video, based on 2025 rate card CPMs of **$18 to $96 per 1,000 views** ([VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/travel)). These are modeled ranges, not closed deals, so treat them as a starting point for a quote.

## Travel rates by subscriber count

Modeled dedicated video rate for a US channel at median engagement, from [VidScored](https://vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates/travel):

| Subscribers | Example avg views | CPM (per 1,000 views) | Dedicated video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 10K | 3,000 | $12 to $64 | $36 to $190 |
| 10K to 100K | 15,000 | $18 to $96 | $270 to $1,400 |
| 100K to 500K | 60,000 | $25 to $135 | $1,500 to $8,100 |
| 500K to 1M | 150,000 | $32 to $175 | $4,800 to $26,000 |
| 1M+ | 400,000 | $42 to $240 | $17,000 to $96,000 |

The same source says real deals vary 2 to 4 times beyond these ranges depending on exclusivity, usage rights, bundles and timing.

## Why travel pricing is seasonal

Travel sponsors pay more in peak windows, around holidays and summer, so a deal booked in a busy period can price well above the same channel's off-season rate. If you can, pitch brands a few months before the season they want to hit.

## Who sponsors travel channels

Booking platforms, luggage and gear brands, tourism boards, travel cards, and eSIM or connectivity services.

## How to price yours

1. Check your average views on the last 10 videos, not your subscriber count.
2. Multiply views by the CPM band for your size, divided by 1,000.
3. Quote the middle of the band, and price usage rights and exclusivity separately.

## FAQ

### How much do travel YouTubers charge for a sponsored video?

About $270 to $1,400 at 10K to 100K subscribers, from $36 for very small channels up to $96,000 and beyond for the largest, per VidScored's modeled rates.

### Are travel rates higher than gaming?

Yes, by these sources. Travel CPMs run $18 to $96 at 10K to 100K subscribers, while published gaming bands run roughly $4 to $26.

### Why do travel sponsorship rates change through the year?

Advertiser budgets peak around holiday and summer travel windows, so peak bookings price higher.
