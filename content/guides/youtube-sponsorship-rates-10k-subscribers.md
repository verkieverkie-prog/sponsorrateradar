# How much do YouTubers get paid for sponsorships with 10K subscribers?

A channel around 10K subscribers can usually get a few hundred dollars for a sponsored segment, and up to about $1,000 if views are strong and the niche pays well. In ThoughtLeaders' data on sold deals, channels under 100K subscribers got a median of $600 per integration, with the middle half of deals between $370 and $1,000.

The honest answer is "it depends on your views," so this guide shows real numbers from small channels and how to work out your own.

## Why views matter more than subscribers

Sponsors buy expected views. 1of10 gives a clear example: a 40,000-subscriber channel averaging 60,000 views per video out-earns a 200,000-subscriber channel averaging 15,000.

So start from views, not subs:

**(Your median views per video / 1,000) x CPM = your rate**

For small channels, the CPM tends to be high. ThoughtLeaders found a median of **$36 per 1,000 projected views** for channels under 100K subscribers, versus $17 for channels over 5M. Brands buying small channels are paying for a focused audience.

### Three quick examples

- **2,000 median views, $30 CPM:** 2 x $30 = $60. That's below most sponsors' minimum effort to set up a deal, so small fixed fees, product-plus-fee, or affiliate deals are common at this level.
- **8,000 median views, $35 CPM:** 8 x $35 = $280.
- **25,000 median views, $30 CPM:** 25 x $30 = $750, right inside the $370-$1,000 band for sub-100K channels.

## What pushes a small channel's rate up

- **Niche.** In [TrendReport's modeled estimates](https://trendreport.com/intelligence/youtube-sponsorship-rates-by-niche/), business and finance runs $18-$47 per 1,000 views, while gaming runs $14-$38. See [finance](/sponsorrateradar/rates/finance.html) and [gaming](/sponsorrateradar/rates/gaming.html).
- **Steady views.** A channel where every video gets about the same views is easier to price than one with a single viral hit.
- **Audience location.** Dangaro notes emerging markets typically see 30-60% lower pricing than the US/English market.
- **Format.** A dedicated video is worth 2x to 4x a 60-90 second integration, per 1of10. A 30-second mention is 0.5x to 0.7x.

## Free product vs cash

At 10K subscribers you'll get a lot of "we'll send you the product for free" offers. 1of10 is direct about it: free product alone is not payment. That doesn't mean always say no. If you'd genuinely use the product and it fits your content, a product deal can be a fine first step. But it's a gift, not a sponsorship rate, and you shouldn't let it set your price.

If a brand offers product in place of a fee, Influencer Advisory suggests writing it on your rate card as "$0 base fee, X dollars retail value of product," so it's clear what's being exchanged.

## What 10K-sub creators should do next

1. **Work out your median views** for the last 10 videos at about 30 days old.
2. **Pick a CPM** from your niche range and size band. $30-$36 is a reasonable starting point for a small channel in a typical niche.
3. **Set a floor.** Decide the lowest cash fee you'll accept before a brand emails you.
4. **Make a one-page rate card** with your integration price, dedicated video price and add-ons.
5. **Raise prices when brands rebook.** ThoughtLeaders found 35% of brands booked the same channel again within a year. A rebooking means your rate was a good deal for them.

## FAQ

### Can you get sponsors with 10K subscribers?

Yes. ThoughtLeaders' sold-deal data includes 1,365 integrations on channels under 100K subscribers, and our database has examples at 3,400 and about 15K subs.

### How much do YouTubers with 1,000 subscribers make from sponsors?

Influencer Marketing Hub lists $20-$200 per sponsored video for 1K-10K subscribers, often as product or performance deals. 1of10 lists $50-$500, often product plus a fee.

### How much is a sponsorship with 50K subscribers?

For channels under 50K subs, Influencer Advisory found a median fee of $1,000. ThoughtLeaders' median for all channels under 100K was $600 per integration.

### Should a small channel charge per view?

Calculate from views, then quote a flat fee. It's simpler for both sides, and you can show the brand your math.
