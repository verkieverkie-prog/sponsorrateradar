# YouTube sponsorship rates per 1,000 views (CPM)

Most YouTube sponsorships in 2026 price out somewhere between $15 and $40 per 1,000 views, with smaller channels at the high end. In 9,002 sold integrations tracked by ThoughtLeaders, the median CPM was $36 for channels under 100K subscribers and $17 for channels over 5M.

Below are the real numbers by channel size and niche, how to use them, and one 2026 change to YouTube view counts that affects the math.

## What "sponsorship CPM" means

CPM stands for cost per mille, or cost per 1,000 views. It's not the same as your AdSense CPM or RPM. It's what a brand pays you, per 1,000 views, for a sponsored segment.

The math in both directions:

- **Price = (expected views / 1,000) x CPM**
- **CPM = price / (expected views / 1,000)**

So a $600 integration on a video expected to get 20,000 views is a $30 CPM. You can run your own numbers in the [sponsorship rate calculator](/sponsorrateradar/calculator.html).

## CPM by channel size (sold deals)

[ThoughtLeaders published data on 9,002 sold YouTube integrations](https://thoughtleaders.ai/blog/2026-10-15-youtube-sponsorship-cost-2026/), measuring cost against each channel's projected views at day 30:

| Channel size | Median cost per 1,000 projected views | Median price |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100K subs | $36 | $600 |
| 100K - 500K subs | $26 | $1,200 |
| 500K - 1M subs | $21 | $2,000 |
| 1M - 5M subs | $19 | $3,500 |
| 5M+ subs | $17 | about $6,000 |

Smaller channels get paid more per view. ThoughtLeaders' read is that brands buying small channels are paying for audience fit, not just reach. Big channels sell volume at a discount.

For big US channels specifically, [Modash's pricing guide](https://www.modash.io/blog/influencer-pricing) lists typical integration CPMs of $30 to $60+.

## CPM by niche

Niche matters as much as size, because brands pay more for viewers who buy expensive things. [TrendReport's modeled estimates](https://trendreport.com/intelligence/youtube-sponsorship-rates-by-niche/), built from sponsored videos it tracked in each category, give these per-1,000-view ranges:

| Niche | Estimated sponsorship CPM | Sponsored videos in sample |
|---|---|---|
| Business and finance | $18 - $47 | 10,375 |
| Education | $16 - $42 | 3,293 |
| Tech and gadgets | $15 - $41 | 11,968 |
| Travel | $15 - $41 | 4,436 |
| Beauty and style | $15 - $40 | 5,446 |
| Food and drink | $15 - $40 | 4,948 |
| Health and wellness | $15 - $40 | 2,307 |
| Lifestyle | $15 - $40 | 4,872 |
| Gaming | $14 - $38 | 19,295 |

These are modeled, not invoices, so treat them as a range to sanity-check against. [1of10's niche table](https://www.1of10.com/blog/youtube-sponsorship-rates/) is more spread out, from $35-$50+ for finance down to $8-$15 for entertainment and vlogs, with gaming at $10-$20.

## What creators report

One creator on r/PartneredYoutube [described their pricing](https://www.reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/comments/zsrm73/how_much_do_you_get_paid_for_sponsorships/) as a flat $20 CPM times the average views of their last ~10 videos. At about 200,000 average views, that came to $4,000 for a 60-second read at the end of the video.

That lines up with the sold-deal data: a $20 CPM is right around the median for channels in the 500K-5M range.

## Which views number to use

The CPM only works if the views number is honest. A few rules sponsors expect:

- **Use recent median views.** Last 10 videos is the common window.
- **Use a fixed age.** ThoughtLeaders measured against views at day 30. Lifetime views or first-day views give very different CPMs.
- **Ignore your one viral video.** Brands will look at your channel page.

### The August 2026 view-count change

On August 24, 2026, YouTube started counting a public view from the first frame. [ThoughtLeaders says](https://thoughtleaders.ai/blog/2026-10-15-youtube-sponsorship-cost-2026/) the old public number is what YouTube Studio now calls engaged views, and that long-form public view counts now run about 1.2 to 1.5 times the old basis in a video's first week. Shorts didn't change.

What this means for you: if a brand compares your quote to today's public view count, your CPM looks cheaper than it really is. When you quote, say which views you mean. Engaged views at day 30 is the cleanest basis, and it matches how the benchmarks above were measured.

## CPM deals vs flat fees

You'll see three structures:

1. **Flat fee.** One price, set up front. Simple. You carry the risk if the video underperforms, the brand carries it if it overperforms.
2. **Pure CPM.** The brand pays per 1,000 views actually delivered, usually counted at 30 days and often with a cap.
3. **Hybrid.** A flat base plus a bonus per signup or sale, common with apps and software, per [1of10](https://www.1of10.com/blog/youtube-sponsorship-rates/).

For most small creators, a flat fee calculated from views x CPM is easiest. It's predictable for you and easy for the brand to approve.

## How to pick your own CPM

1. Start from your size band's median: $36 under 100K subs, $26 at 100K-500K.
2. Nudge it up or down using your niche range. Finance and education can push higher; gaming and entertainment usually sit lower.
3. Nudge up if your views are steady and your audience is mostly in high-income countries.
4. Multiply by your median 30-day views and round to a clean number.

Then check the result against the [calculator](/sponsorrateradar/calculator.html) and the [rates pages](/sponsorrateradar/rates/).

## FAQ

### What is the average YouTube sponsorship rate per 1,000 views?

In ThoughtLeaders' sold-deal data, median CPMs ranged from $36 (under 100K subs) to $17 (5M+ subs). Most niche estimates fall between $15 and $40.

### Is sponsorship CPM the same as AdSense CPM?

No. AdSense CPM is what advertisers pay YouTube for ads on your video. Sponsorship CPM is what a brand pays you directly for a segment you make. They're separate income streams.

### Why do small channels get a higher CPM?

ThoughtLeaders' data shows price grows much slower than channel size, so cost per view falls as channels get bigger. Small channels are often bought for audience fit rather than raw reach.

### Should I use public views or engaged views?

Since YouTube's August 24, 2026 view-count change, public long-form views run higher than the old count. Quote in engaged views at a fixed age, like day 30, so your CPM compares fairly to benchmarks.

### Which niche has the highest sponsorship CPM?

Business and finance has the highest range in TrendReport's estimates ($18-$47 per 1,000 views), and 1of10 also puts finance at the top.
